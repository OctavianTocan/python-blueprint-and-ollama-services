## Blueprint Summary

**Purpose**: Melee combat component for hit detection, damage application, and physics impulse in third-person character combat

**Key Functions**:
- `EventGraph` - Main melee execution flow with damage, visual effects, and server replication
- `CGraph Act` - Character viewpoint-based trace endpoint calculation for accurate hit detection
- `CGraph Add Impulse` - Physics impulse application to hit targets
